# Echoes of Alderfall

## Main Story Questline: **The Twelve Socket Stones**

## Core Premise

Alderfall is not a shining heroic realm. It is a wounded land held together by old roads, tired ward-stones, market bells, border towers, village oaths, and people who still remember how the world is supposed to work.

Then the Demon Queen returns.

At the beginning of the game, the player confronts the Demon Queen during a failed attempt to stop a dark ritual. The player is defeated completely. The Demon Queen spares them, not out of kindness, but as an act of cruelty.

She calls it mercy.

The player survives and wakes near **Oathstead Camp**, a half-ruined frontier settlement that becomes their base. From there, the player must travel across the five kingdoms of Alderfall, gather proof of the Demon Queen’s return, win the trust of powerful figures, rebuild faith between settlements, and collect **twelve socket stones** needed to activate an ancient portal.

The portal is the only known path to the Demon Queen’s domain.

But Alderfall is fractured. The kingdoms distrust each other. Roads are unsafe. Ward-stones are failing. Records have been lost or deliberately hidden. Villages care more about bread, medicine, bells, wells, bridges, and raiders than ancient prophecies.

The player cannot simply announce that the world is ending and expect everyone to listen.

They must prove it.

All main story NPCs should have **red exclamation marks** above their heads.

---

# Main Story Structure

The main story is divided into five major acts:

1. **Act I — The Queen’s Mercy**
   The player survives defeat and begins gathering proof.

2. **Act II — The Broken Roads**
   The player travels between kingdoms, earning trust from local leaders.

3. **Act III — The Twelve Socket Stones**
   The player learns how to locate and collect the twelve stones.

4. **Act IV — Oathstead Rises**
   The player rebuilds Oathstead into a functioning settlement and rally point.

5. **Act V — The Old Portal Opens**
   The player finds the ancient portal, activates it, and confronts the Demon Queen.

# Key Main Story NPCs

## Maelis of Oathstead

**Location:** Oathstead Camp
**Role:** Settlement leader and first main story guide
**Quest Marker:** Red exclamation mark

Maelis is one of the first people to find the player after the Demon Queen’s attack. She is practical, tired, and more concerned with keeping people alive than chasing legends. She does not fully understand what the player saw, but she believes fear when she sees it.

Maelis becomes the player’s first anchor in Alderfall. Through her, Oathstead Camp becomes more than a resting place. It becomes the heart of the resistance.

### Quest Chain: “Mercy Is Not Mercy”

#### Quest 1: Wake Among Ashes

**Type:** visit/inspect

The player wakes near Oathstead Camp, wounded and barely alive.

**Objectives:**

* Visit Maelis at Oathstead Camp.
* Inspect the burned road shrine.
* Inspect the broken ward-stone near camp.
* Return to Maelis.

**Story Result:**
Maelis realizes the attack was not a normal monster raid. The ward-stone did not fail naturally — it was corrupted.

#### Quest 2: Proof in the Road Dust

**Type:** gather

Maelis asks the player to gather proof before she risks spreading panic.

**Objectives:**

* Gather 3x Demon Ash from the burned road.
* Gather 2x Broken Ward Fragments.
* Gather 1x Charred Banner from the battlefield.

**Story Result:**
Maelis sends the player to Archive City, where old royal records may explain what the Demon Queen wants.

#### Quest 3: Oathstead Must Stand

**Type:** gather / defeat

Before the player leaves, Oathstead needs supplies.

**Objectives:**

* Gather 5x Crates.
* Gather 5x Wheat.
* Defeat 6x Wolves near the camp road.

**Story Result:**
Oathstead becomes the player’s official base. Future settlement upgrades and workers can be introduced here.

---

## Selene of Archive City

**Location:** Archive City, Crownlands of Alderfall
**Role:** Royal archivist and keeper of hidden histories
**Quest Marker:** Red exclamation mark

Selene is responsible for preserving Alderfall’s royal records, but the archives are damaged, incomplete, and politically censored. She knows the Demon Queen existed, but admitting that publicly would expose generations of royal lies.

She will not help the player until they prove they are not working for one of the kingdoms trying to control the truth.

### Quest Chain: “The Records That Lied”

#### Quest 1: Names Under Dust

**Type:** visit/inspect

Selene asks the player to inspect damaged archive rooms.

**Objectives:**

* Inspect the Burned Royal Ledger.
* Inspect the sealed oath cabinet.
* Inspect the missing section of the Demon War records.

**Story Result:**
The player discovers that the Demon Queen was once fought using twelve socket stones.

#### Quest 2: The Stolen Index

**Type:** defeat / gather

Bandits have stolen archive pages and are hiding near **Crowhook Bandit Camp**.

**Objectives:**

* Defeat 8x Bandits.
* Gather 4x Stolen Archive Pages.
* Return to Selene.

**Story Result:**
Selene reconstructs part of the old portal record.

#### Quest 3: The First Socket

**Type:** visit/inspect

Selene sends the player below Archive City into a sealed record vault.

**Objectives:**

* Visit the Old Oath Vault.
* Inspect the royal socket mural.
* Inspect the empty stone pedestal.

**Reward:**
The player receives the first major clue: there are twelve stones, and they were distributed across Alderfall to prevent any one kingdom from controlling the portal.

### Socket Stone Reward

**Stone of Memory**
**Acquisition:** Found in the sealed archive vault beneath Archive City.
**Condition:** Only appears after Selene’s trust chain is complete.

---

## Odrick Ironvale

**Location:** Ironvale Town / Highwall City, Highwall March
**Role:** Veteran commander and roadwatch leader
**Quest Marker:** Red exclamation mark

Odrick is a soldier of Highwall March. He is not interested in old myths unless they threaten roads, walls, and people under his protection. Highwall is under pressure from raiders, orcs, and frost-road monsters.

To earn his trust, the player must prove they can protect more than themselves.

### Quest Chain: “Iron on the Frost Road”

#### Quest 1: Watchtower Without Bells

**Type:** visit/inspect

A Highwall watchtower has gone silent.

**Objectives:**

* Visit the frozen watchtower.
* Inspect the dead campfire.
* Inspect the broken signal bell.
* Inspect the blood marks near the palisade.

**Story Result:**
Odrick learns the attack was coordinated by something smarter than raiders.

#### Quest 2: Raiders at the Pass

**Type:** defeat

The player must clear raiders blocking the mountain road.

**Objectives:**

* Defeat 10x Raiders near Highwall Pass.
* Defeat 3x Orc Brutes.
* Return to Odrick.

**Story Result:**
Odrick begins to trust the player and reveals that Highwall once guarded one of the socket stones.

#### Quest 3: Frosthollow Standard

**Type:** defeat

A demon servant has taken control of **Frosthollow Cave** and is rallying raiders under a black war banner.

**Boss:** Kharvok the Banner-Bound

Kharvok is one of the Demon Queen’s servants, an armored war demon bound to a burning banner. He does not fight alone.

**Boss Fight Structure:**

* Phase 1: Kharvok uses heavy melee attacks.
* Phase 2: He summons raiders and orcs.
* Phase 3: He plants the black banner, strengthening all enemies until the player destroys it.

### Socket Stone Reward

**Stone of Iron**
**Acquisition:** Dropped by Kharvok.
**Condition:** The stone only drops if Odrick’s boss quest is active.

---

## Solari of Sanctum City

**Location:** Sanctum City, Sanctum Sunrealm
**Role:** Sun priestess and keeper of ember rites
**Quest Marker:** Red exclamation mark

Solari protects the desert shrines of the Sanctum Sunrealm. She understands old ward-stones better than most living scholars. She believes the Demon Queen’s return is connected to the weakening of ancient sunlight wards buried beneath the desert.

Solari is calm and severe. She does not give knowledge freely, because ember magic can destroy as easily as it can protect.

### Quest Chain: “Embers Beneath the Sand”

#### Quest 1: Shrine Without Shadow

**Type:** visit/inspect

Solari asks the player to inspect a desert shrine where shadows behave unnaturally.

**Objectives:**

* Visit the Sunken Shrine.
* Inspect the cracked sun altar.
* Inspect the dead caravan camels.
* Inspect the old ward-stone.

**Story Result:**
The player discovers that demon corruption is draining ember magic.

#### Quest 2: Caravan of Glass

**Type:** gather / defeat

A caravan carrying shrine supplies vanished in the badlands.

**Objectives:**

* Defeat 6x Ember Imps.
* Gather 4x Shrine Crates.
* Gather 2x Sun Oil Flasks.

**Story Result:**
Solari agrees to teach the player part of an old crafting rite.

#### Quest 3: The Ember Socket Rite

**Type:** gather

Solari teaches the player how to craft one socket stone using desert materials.

**Objectives:**

* Gather 3x Ember Crystal.
* Gather 2x Sunsteppe Glass.
* Gather 1x Demon Ash.
* Return to the Sanctum forge shrine.

### Socket Stone Reward

**Stone of Ember**
**Acquisition:** Crafted at a shrine forge in Sanctum City.
**Condition:** Recipe is unlocked only after Solari’s trust chain is complete.

---

## Ysra of Belltower City

**Location:** Belltower City, Belltower Fenlands
**Role:** Bellwarden of the marshes
**Quest Marker:** Red exclamation mark

Ysra maintains the marsh bells that guide travelers through the fog. In the Fenlands, bells are not decorative. They keep boats from vanishing, warn villages of bog beasts, and mark safe paths across reed causeways.

Recently, some bells have begun ringing by themselves.

Ysra suspects the dead are restless beneath the water.

### Quest Chain: “When the Marsh Bells Ring”

#### Quest 1: The Bell That Rang Alone

**Type:** visit/inspect

Ysra asks the player to inspect a bell tower outside Belltower City.

**Objectives:**

* Visit the marsh bell tower.
* Inspect the bell rope.
* Inspect the muddy footprints.
* Inspect the reed shrine.

**Story Result:**
The player discovers that the bell was rung by something dead.

#### Quest 2: Medicine for Mireford

**Type:** gather

Before Ysra shares more, she asks the player to help **Mireford Village**, where marsh fever is spreading.

**Objectives:**

* Gather 6x Fever Reed.
* Gather 3x Clean Water Skins.
* Deliver them to Mireford Village.

**Story Result:**
Ysra trusts that the player cares about people, not just prophecy.

#### Quest 3: Miredepth Below

**Type:** defeat

Ysra sends the player into **Miredepth Cave**, where a demon-touched bog creature is nesting.

**Boss:** Velmora, the Bell-Drowned

Velmora is a drowned wraith-beast covered in ropes, bells, and reed charms.

**Boss Fight Structure:**

* Phase 1: Uses water and fog attacks.
* Phase 2: Summons drowned villagers.
* Phase 3: Rings cursed bells that confuse the player unless the bells are destroyed.

### Socket Stone Reward

**Stone of Tides**
**Acquisition:** Found inside Velmora’s flooded lair.
**Condition:** The boss and stone are only available after Ysra opens the path to Miredepth Cave.

---

## Mirella of Riverside City

**Location:** Riverside City, Riverside Reach
**Role:** River noble, negotiator, and trade authority
**Quest Marker:** Red exclamation mark

Mirella is one of the few river nobles who understands that Alderfall is close to collapse. Trade routes are failing, bridge keeps are fighting over tolls, and river barons are blaming each other for raids.

She has information about a socket stone that passed through the river markets, but she will not risk civil disorder unless the player helps stabilize the Reach.

### Quest Chain: “Bridges Before Crowns”

#### Quest 1: The Toll Ledger

**Type:** visit/inspect

Mirella asks the player to inspect a suspicious toll ledger at a bridge keep.

**Objectives:**

* Visit Briarbridge Town.
* Inspect the toll ledger.
* Inspect the empty supply crates.
* Inspect the bridge guard post.

**Story Result:**
The player discovers that supplies are being redirected to a hidden buyer.

#### Quest 2: Redcap Trade

**Type:** defeat / gather

The hidden buyer is connected to **Redcap Goblin Camp**.

**Objectives:**

* Defeat 8x Redcap Goblins.
* Gather 5x Stolen Supply Crates.
* Return to Mirella.

**Story Result:**
Mirella reveals that goblins found a “socket stone” among old battlefield bones.

#### Quest 3: Glowing Thing in the Mud

**Type:** defeat / gather

The player must search goblin corpses in a marked battlefield zone.

**Objectives:**

* Defeat Redcap Goblin scavengers.
* Gather 1x Mud-Caked Socket Stone from goblin corpses.

### Socket Stone Reward

**Stone of Hunger**
**Acquisition:** Found on a goblin corpse.
**Condition:** Goblin corpses cannot drop this stone until Mirella’s quest is active.

# Portal Discovery

## Oracle Event: “The Place Between Roads”

After the player collects enough proof and several socket stones, Selene, Solari, Ysra, Odrick, Mirella, and Maelis combine their knowledge.

They discover that the portal is not in a royal palace, temple, or fortress.

It is hidden in an old road crossing between the five kingdoms — a forgotten neutral place where the first oaths of Alderfall were sworn.

The location appears on the world map as:

**The Old Gate of Alderfall**

This should be a unique world location with a large ruined portal asset. The portal should look ancient, cracked, and inactive until all twelve socket stones are inserted.

# Final Stone: Stone of Dawn

The final stone should be awarded only after the player has gained the trust of all five major kingdoms.

## Quest: “The Kingdoms Answer”

**Quest Giver:** Maelis
**Type:** visit/inspect

### Objectives:

* Visit Riverside City and speak with Mirella.
* Visit Highwall City and speak with Odrick.
* Visit Archive City and speak with Selene.
* Visit Belltower City and speak with Ysra.
* Visit Sanctum City and speak with Solari.
* Return to Oathstead Camp.

Each leader agrees to support Oathstead, not because they fully trust each other, but because they trust what the player has done.

### Reward:

**Stone of Dawn**

The Stone of Dawn is formed from five royal oath fragments, one from each kingdom.

# Portal Activation Scene

When the player inserts the twelve socket stones, the portal awakens.

The stones do not simply power the portal. They replay echoes of Alderfall’s history:

* The first ward being raised.
* The first road oath being sworn.
* The first betrayal between kingdoms.
* The original sealing of the Demon Queen.
* The Demon Queen’s voice calling from the Hollow Throne.

Then Vaelthara speaks through the portal.

## Demon Queen Dialogue

**Vaelthara:**
“So. The little survivor gathers stones and calls it courage.”

**Player:**
“I survived you once.”

**Vaelthara:**
“No. I permitted your breathing. There is a difference.”

**Maelis:**
“You should have killed them.”

**Vaelthara:**
“I know.”

The portal opens.

The final dungeon becomes available:

**The Hollow Throne**

---

# Final Dungeon: The Hollow Throne

The Hollow Throne is the Demon Queen’s domain. It should feel different from the rest of Alderfall. While Alderfall is grounded in roads, bells, mud, ledgers, villages, and old stones, the Hollow Throne is symbolic and broken.

Possible areas:

* The Mercy Hall
* The Court of Ash
* The Road of Failed Oaths
* The Throne Without Sky

## Final Boss: Vaelthara, Demon Queen of Mercy

Vaelthara should be a proper multi-phase boss fight.

### Phase 1: The Queen’s Mercy

Vaelthara fights alone.

**Mechanics:**

* Wide sword or claw attacks.
* Dark flame projectiles.
* Teleporting behind the player.
* Dialogue mocking the player’s survival.

### Phase 2: The Twelve Broken Oaths

Vaelthara summons echoes of corrupted kingdom symbols.

**Mechanics:**

* Riverside chains slow the player.
* Highwall shields block parts of the arena.
* Archive pages become cutting projectiles.
* Fenland fog reduces visibility.
* Sanctum fire creates burning zones.

### Phase 3: No More Mercy

Vaelthara becomes enraged and breaks part of the arena.

**Mechanics:**

* Faster attacks.
* Summons lesser demons.
* Uses a devastating attack that must be interrupted by activating socket-stone echoes around the arena.

### Final Moment

The player does not defeat Vaelthara through strength alone. The twelve stones respond to the trust rebuilt across Alderfall.

The portal does not simply close.

It rejects her.

Vaelthara is pulled back into the Hollow Throne, weakened but not necessarily destroyed. This leaves room for future expansions or post-game content.

---

# Ending

After Vaelthara falls, Alderfall is not magically fixed.

The roads are still damaged. The dead still need burying. The wards still need repair. The kingdoms still distrust each other.

But something has changed.

Oathstead Camp is no longer just a camp.

It is the first place in Alderfall where the five kingdoms chose to stand together.

The final scene shows villagers, soldiers, priests, traders, bellwardens, scribes, and refugees working side by side.

The player stands near the oath marker.

Maelis says:

> “Alderfall was never saved by crowns. It was saved by people who kept their promises.”

The game can then continue into post-game settlement rebuilding, remaining monster bounties, companion quests, dungeon cleanup, and restoring the old wards.

# Implementation Notes For Quest Design

## Main Story Quest Marker

All NPCs connected to the main story should use:

**Red exclamation mark**

Examples:

* Maelis
* Selene
* Odrick
* Solari
* Ysra
* Mirella
* Elder Rowan
* Gravekeeper Hollis
* Captain Elric
* Bellwright Nessa
* Ash-Scribe Damar

## Supported Objective Types

Since the current quest system supports **defeat**, **gather**, and **visit/inspect**, the story should avoid objectives that require complex mechanics unless they can be represented through those types.

Examples:

**Dialogue quest becomes:**

* Visit NPC
* Inspect object
* Return to NPC

**Investigation quest becomes:**

* Inspect ledger
* Inspect campfire
* Inspect ward seal

**Dungeon quest becomes:**

* Defeat enemies
* Gather item
* Inspect final altar

**Crafting quest becomes:**

* Gather ingredients
* Visit forge/shrine/anvil
* Receive item

**Trust quest becomes:**

* Complete a chain of local practical tasks
* Unlock new dialogue
* Reveal socket stone clue

## Conditional Drops

Some important story items should not exist until the related quest is accepted.

Examples:

* Stone of Hunger only drops from goblins after Mirella’s Redcap quest is active.
* Stone of Iron only drops from Kharvok after Odrick sends the player to Frosthollow.
* Stone of Ash only drops from Sareth after the Blackvault quest is active.
* Stone of Tides only appears after Ysra opens the Miredepth route.

This prevents the player from accidentally collecting main story stones too early.

## Settlement Integration

Oathstead Camp should grow alongside the main quest.

Possible stages:

### Stage 1: Survivor Camp

Unlocked after “Wake Among Ashes.”

Features:

* Basic rest point
* Maelis available
* Small quest board

### Stage 2: Working Camp

Unlocked after “Oathstead Must Stand.”

Features:

* Storage crates
* First workers
* Basic crafting area

### Stage 3: Defended Settlement

Unlocked after collecting several socket stones.

Features:

* Palisades
* Guards
* More NPCs
* Companion gathering point

### Stage 4: Oathstead Hold

Unlocked after “A Camp Worth Defending.”

Features:

* Central oath marker
* Improved buildings
* Kingdom representatives
* Final main story preparation

# Main Story Summary

The story of Echoes of Alderfall is not only about defeating the Demon Queen.

It is about proving that the world is still worth saving.

The player begins as someone spared and humiliated by Vaelthara. By traveling across Alderfall, helping towns with practical problems, rebuilding trust, recovering lost records, repairing local systems, and collecting the twelve socket stones, the player becomes the one person capable of uniting the fractured realm.

The Demon Queen believes mercy is weakness.

The player proves that mercy, trust, memory, and oaths are the only reasons Alderfall survives.
