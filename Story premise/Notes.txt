Strong Ideas

Companion Memory Journal++
Track recent notable events per companion:
fought beside player
player was defeated
player healed them
player ignored their quest
assigned to a building
entered their home region
completed a main story milestone
Then dialogue can say:
"You came back to Oakhaven before I had to ask. I noticed."

Companion Approval Profiles++
Each companion likes/dislikes different choices:
Cassia likes duty, honesty, protecting civilians.
Seraphine likes discretion, anti-noble defiance, freedom.
Lyra likes mercy, supplies, healing, avoiding cruelty.
Rafiq likes boldness, style, second chances.
Same player answer could affect companions differently.

Companion-to-Companion Relationships
Let companions slowly form opinions of each other.
Example:
Cassia and Rafiq start tense.
Lyra and Cassia bond over battlefield guilt.
Maera and Samir debate faith vs records.
Aria and Seraphine respect each other’s guardedness.
This can unlock banter chains.

Campfire Conversations++
At Oathstead or inns, add longer “rest conversations.”
Player chooses who to sit with:
ask about past
ask about another companion
discuss the Demon Queen
share a quiet moment
flirt / deepen romance if available

Companion Quest Epilogues++
After stage 8, give each companion 2-3 small post-quest scenes:
Seraphine rewrites fair contracts for Oathstead.
Maera builds the map table.
Cassia trains gate volunteers.
Lyra starts a mobile clinic.
Calder fixes a bridge and admits he slept well.

Contextual Exploration Barks
Companions comment based on biome/location:
Aria in forest: trail/trap comments.
Samir in Sanctum: conflicted faith comments.
Maera in Archive: old-record comments.
Vesper in snow: root/survival comments.

Combat Personality Barks
Each companion reacts differently:
low HP
killing blow
player heals them
companion protects player
boss appears
victory
Example:
Lyra: "You are not allowed to die while I am still this annoyed."

Player Reputation Themes
Track what kind of player the player is becoming:
merciful
ruthless
practical
curious
loyal
greedy
reckless
Companions comment:
"You keep choosing mercy even when it costs. That is either courage or a wound."

Companion Requests++
Small repeatable requests:
Lyra asks for bandages.
Calder asks for timber.
Maera asks for notes.
Aria asks to scout a road.
Samir asks to light a shrine.
Completing them gives small trust and banter.

Relationship Milestone Scenes
At trust thresholds, trigger a short scene:
50: guarded respect
100: personal admission
150: loyalty
180: romance possible
250: marriage/future conversation



After that, I’d do quest stage dialogue locks: each quest dialogue branch should know whether it is offer, accepted, progress, ready, or completed, and only show lines/actions for that state. This would prevent “quest talk everywhere” from creeping back.

Third, I’d add companion-specific milestone variants for 150, 180, and 250. We already have personal 50/100 lines; loyalty, romance, and future are currently more shared. Making those later scenes companion-specific would make attachment land harder.

My pick: do root dialogue priority/grouping next. It will immediately improve the feel of every conversation screen.


Ideas I’d consider next: make date memories affect future banter, 
add small companion-specific jealousy/awkwardness lines if multiple romances are active, 
and add “anniversary” style callbacks after major story victories or returning to Oathstead.
