Dialogue Options and Flow
=========================

Purpose
-------

This document describes how the current dialogue system flows after the layered companion dialogue update.

The important design rule is:

Dialogue should feel like a branching tree. The player starts at a trunk topic, moves onto one branch, then chooses smaller sticks on that branch: clarify, personal meaning, practical task, challenge, support, aftermath.

This document focuses on companion dialogue because that is where attachment, trust, recruitment, romance, Oathstead life, and building assignments matter most.


Global Dialogue Structure
-------------------------

Every dialogue session starts at a root line.

For normal NPCs, the root line is usually picked from their stored NPC dialogue. Their topics remain simple.

For special companion NPCs, the root line is chosen from:

1. Married state.
2. Romanced state.
3. Stationed at Oathstead.
4. Active companion quest state.
5. Relationship level.
6. Guarded fallback.

Companion root topics can include:

1. Active quest branch, if the companion has a current companion quest.
2. Relationship/trust branch.
3. Oathstead branch, if recruited or stationed.
4. Local/place branch.


Companion State Inputs
----------------------

Companion dialogue can react to these saved game states:

* recruited
* stationed at Oathstead
* romanced
* married
* assigned Oathstead building
* assigned worker role
* relationship level
* quest accepted / in progress / ready / completed
* companion quest stage 1-8

Village assignment and companion relationship states are saved through:

* villageAllies
* romancedCompanions
* marriedCompanions
* villageWorkerRoles
* villageBuildingAssignments


Companion Quest Branch
----------------------

This branch appears when the NPC has an active companion quest.

Root option label depends on companion and quest stage:

* Stage 7: "Why does this fight matter?"
* Stage 8: "What will you choose now?"
* Otherwise: companion-specific quest topic.

Companion-specific quest topics:

* Seraphine: "What are you really stealing back?"
* Maera: "Why is this map forbidden?"
* Cassia: "What happened at the gate?"
* Lyra: "Who needs help at the clinic?"
* Samir: "Why is the reliquary burning?"
* Aria: "What happened to your sister?"
* Vesper: "What is hidden under the snow?"
* Rafiq: "Who wants you dead?"
* Calder: "Why is the bridge failing?"


Quest Opening Text
------------------

The NPC opening changes based on quest state:

* Not accepted: use quest start dialogue.
* Accepted, no progress: use stage-aware quest greeting.
* Accepted, partial progress: use progress dialogue plus "Progress is proof that the story can still be changed."
* Ready to complete: use ready dialogue plus stage-specific readiness line.
* Completed: use completion dialogue plus stage-specific aftermath line.

Stage readiness lines:

* Stage 5 or 6: "I am ready to hear the part I avoided."
* Stage 7: "Now the fight has to become an ending."
* Stage 8: "Now I choose what comes after."
* Other stages: "Bring it here. We will make the next choice with eyes open."

Stage aftermath lines:

* Stage 1: "That was the first honest mark on a very dishonest road."
* Stage 2: "Useful help is how trust learns to stand."
* Stage 3: "Evidence has a colder kindness than comfort. It does not look away."
* Stage 4: "The old wound has enemies now. That means it can be fought."
* Stage 5: "You heard the truth and did not try to make it prettier. I noticed."
* Stage 6: "I am still afraid. I am also still here. That distinction matters."
* Stage 7: "The thing that hunted me has an ending now."
* Stage 8: "Loyalty is not a chain. It is a road I am choosing with both feet."


Quest Branch Options
--------------------

When the player selects the active companion quest topic, the NPC gives the current quest opening. Then the player sees:

1. "What is really happening?"
   Relationship: +1
   Goes to: Clarify node.

2. Companion-specific personal question.
   Relationship: +1
   Goes to: Personal node.

3. Practical objective question.
   Relationship: +1
   Goes to: Practical node.

4. "Can we skip the story and do the work?"
   Relationship: -1
   Goes to: Pushback node.

5. "Back to topics"
   Relationship: 0
   Goes to: root.


Practical Objective Question Labels
-----------------------------------

The third option changes by objective type:

* Defeat objective: "What are we fighting?"
* Gather objective: "What should I gather?"
* Visit objective: "What should I inspect?"


Clarify Node
------------

The NPC explains the actual meaning of the quest stage.

Follow-up options:

1. "And what does that cost you?"
   Relationship: +1
   Goes to: Personal node.

2. Practical objective question.
   Relationship: +1
   Goes to: Practical node.

3. "I will keep the thread straight."
   Relationship: +2
   Goes to: Support response.

4. "Back to topics"
   Relationship: 0
   Goes to: root.


Personal Node
-------------

The NPC explains why the quest matters personally.

Follow-up options:

1. "I will carry that carefully."
   Relationship: +2
   Goes to: Support response.

2. "Tell me only what helps the work."
   Relationship: 0
   Goes to: Practical node.

3. "That sounds like an excuse."
   Relationship: -2
   Goes to: Challenge response.

4. "Back to topics"
   Relationship: 0
   Goes to: root.


Practical Node
--------------

The NPC explains what the player should actually do.

Follow-up options:

1. "I will bring proof, not guesses."
   Relationship: +1
   Goes to: Support response.

2. "What could go wrong?"
   Relationship: +1
   Goes to: Warning response.

3. "Back to topics"
   Relationship: 0
   Goes to: root.


Pushback Node
-------------

This is used when the player rushes the companion.

Player option:

"Can we skip the story and do the work?"

NPC response:

"You can. But then you will only know where to swing, not why the wound matters."

Follow-up options:

1. "You are right. Tell me the why."
   Relationship: +1
   Goes to: Personal node.

2. "Then give me the clean task."
   Relationship: 0
   Goes to: Practical node.

3. "Back to topics"
   Relationship: 0
   Goes to: root.


Support Response
----------------

This ends the small branch and returns to topic choice.

Stage-dependent result:

* Stage 5, 6, or 7:
  "Good. I do not need rescuing from the truth. I need someone beside me while I stop obeying it."

* Stage 8:
  Companion speaks about Oathstead/home/truth.

* Other stages:
  "That is the right kind of help. Not loud. Not possessive. Present."


Challenge Response
------------------

This ends the small branch and returns to topic choice.

Stage-dependent result:

* Stage 5 or 6:
  "Maybe. But pain can be true and still not be an excuse. I am trying to learn the difference."

* Stage 7:
  Companion pushes back against reducing the loyalty fight to "just another fight."

* Other stages:
  "You can doubt me. Just do it with open eyes, not impatience wearing armor."


Warning Response
----------------

This ends the small branch and returns to topic choice.

Stage-dependent result:

* Stage 3:
  "Evidence may be planted. Look for what is too neat, too clean, or too eager to be found."

* Stage 4 or 7:
  Companion gives tactical warning.

* Stage 8:
  "Old endings sometimes pretend to be peace. Watch for what still asks to be carried."

* Other stages:
  "The danger is not only the task. It is believing the task is small because the first step is simple."


Quest Stage Meaning
-------------------

Stage 1: First Contact

Purpose:
Introduce the companion's problem and voice.

Clarify line:
Uses the companion's first quest opening and origin line.

Personal line:
Uses the companion-specific personal response.

Practical line:
"Start where the first lie touches the ground. Bring back proof, not guesses."


Stage 2: Practical Help

Purpose:
Ground the relationship in useful work.

Clarify line:
"This is the practical part, where pretty motives become sore hands."

Personal line:
"I am asking for grounded help because trust has to survive ordinary weight before it survives danger."

Practical line:
"Small things matter because someone is counting on us to overlook them. Bring back proof, not guesses."


Stage 3: Investigation

Purpose:
Let objects and locations reveal the companion's deeper issue.

Clarify line:
"Investigation is where the past stops being polite. Objects remember what people edit."

Personal line:
"I need a witness because I know how easily one person can talk themselves around an inconvenient truth."

Practical line:
"Let objects contradict people. Objects are less ambitious. Bring back proof, not guesses."


Stage 4: Conflict

Purpose:
Enemies tied to the companion's past enter the story.

Clarify line:
"The conflict has names, weapons, and timing. That means it can be faced, but it is no longer only mine."

Personal line:
"The fight matters because enemies connected to the old wound are trying to decide my future for me."

Practical line:
"Enemies protect the weak point. Notice what they guard hardest. Bring back proof, not guesses."


Stage 5: Backstory Reveal

Purpose:
Companion admits guilt, debt, failure, doubt, betrayal, exile, shame, or grief.

Clarify line:
"This is the backstory part. I dislike that phrase. It makes old pain sound decorative."

Personal line:
Uses the companion wound line.

Practical line:
"Do not polish what you find. I need the ugly version. Bring back proof, not guesses."


Stage 6: Personal Trial

Purpose:
Companion faces the core wound directly.

Clarify line:
"A trial is not a test of whether I hurt. It is a test of whether hurt still gets to command me."

Personal line:
"If I face this alone, I will call it independence and mean fear. I would rather be more honest than that."

Practical line:
"If I hesitate, do not mistake it for surrender. Bring back proof, not guesses."


Stage 7: Loyalty Quest

Purpose:
Boss, dangerous site, or major confrontation.

Clarify line:
Companion explains what the fight really means.

Personal line:
Companion admits trust in the player.

Practical line:
"Win the opening, not the argument. Bring back proof, not guesses."


Stage 8: Aftermath / Recruitment / Loyalty Upgrade

Purpose:
Companion chooses Oathstead, the player, or a new life.

Clarify line:
Companion states their final choice.

Personal line:
Companion says what they need from the player.

Practical line:
"We are not erasing the past. We are choosing where it stops. Bring back proof, not guesses."

Relationship / Trust Branch
---------------------------

This branch is always available for companions.

Root topic label depends on relationship state:

* Married: "Talk about your promise"
* Romanced or relationship 180+: "Talk about what is between you"
* Relationship 120+: "Talk about trust"
* Relationship 50+: "Talk more personally"
* Lower relationship: "Earn their trust"


Married State
-------------

Opening:
Companion marriage opening.

Options:

1. "Come home to me when you can."
   Relationship: +2
   Result: warm romance response.

2. "Tell me what still frightens you."
   Relationship: +2
   Result: personal truth response.

3. "The work comes first today."
   Relationship: 0
   Result: mission-focused response.

4. "Back to topics"
   Relationship: 0
   Result: root.


Marriage-Ready State
--------------------

Condition:
Relationship 250+ and not married.

Opening:
Companion marriage opening.

Options:

1. "Marry me, [short name]."
   Relationship: +5
   Effect: marriage:accept
   Result: companion accepts proposal.

2. "Would you want a future with me?"
   Relationship: +2
   Result: companion admits wanting it.

3. "One day, when the road is quieter."
   Relationship: +1
   Result: companion accepts later, but not never.

4. "I cannot promise forever."
   Relationship: -1
   Result: companion asks for truth instead of uncertain kindness.


Romance State
-------------

Condition:
Romanced, or relationship 180+.

Opening:
Companion loving opening.

Options:

1. "I feel it too."
   Relationship: +3
   Effect: romance:start
   Result: romance deepens.

2. "Stay with me a while."
   Relationship: +3
   Effect: romance:start
   Result: romance deepens.

3. "This matters to me."
   Relationship: +2
   Result: warm response.

4. "We should keep this professional."
   Relationship: -2
   Effect: romance:end
   Result: romance ends / companion gives space.


Loyal State
-----------

Condition:
Relationship 150+.

Opening:
"You have become harder to leave behind. That is either trust or poor tactical judgment."

Options:

1. "Stand with me."
   Relationship: +2
   Result: companion confirms they already are.

2. "You are free to choose your road."
   Relationship: +2
   Result: companion appreciates choice.

3. "I am glad you are here."
   Relationship: +1
   Result: warm response.

4. "Good. I need your skills."
   Relationship: -1
   Result: companion notes warmth is not inventory.


Trusted State
-------------

Condition:
Relationship 120+.

Opening:
"There is a truth I would not hand to a stranger. You are no longer convenient enough to be one."

Options:

1. "Tell me the full truth."
   Relationship: +2
   Result: companion personal response.

2. "I will carry this carefully."
   Relationship: +2
   Result: companion asks that truth not be carried like a trophy.

3. "You trust me that much?"
   Relationship: +1
   Result: companion admits trust.

4. "Would someone else be safer?"
   Relationship: -1
   Result: companion says safer perhaps, truer no.


Friend State
------------

Condition:
Relationship 90+.

Opening:
"You have a habit of being present before I decide whether I need anyone present."

Options:

1. "I am listening."
   Relationship: +2
   Result: companion values listening without immediate advice.

2. "Take your time."
   Relationship: +2
   Result: companion values time without pressure.

3. "Can it help us?"
   Relationship: +1
   Result: companion pushes against reducing everything to mission value.

4. "Make it quick."
   Relationship: -1
   Result: companion says quick stories are for taverns.


Friendly State
--------------

Condition:
Relationship 50+.

Opening:
"I am beginning to believe you are useful on purpose."

Options:

1. "Good to see you too."
   Relationship: +2
   Result: companion may grow used to being greeted like a person.

2. "You sound almost comfortable."
   Relationship: +1
   Result: companion warns not to startle comfort.

3. "What changed?"
   Relationship: +1
   Result: companion says the player kept showing up.

4. "Stay alert."
   Relationship: -1
   Result: companion says alert and alone are not the same.


Acquaintance State
------------------

Condition:
Relationship 20+.

Opening:
"We are not friends yet. We are two people with overlapping problems and tolerable timing."

Options:

1. "I will take useful for now."
   Relationship: +1
   Result: companion accepts useful as a respectable beginning.

2. "I would rather earn trust."
   Relationship: +2
   Result: companion respects earned trust.

3. "You keep your distance."
   Relationship: 0
   Result: companion says distance can allow honest approach.

4. "I do not need your trust."
   Relationship: -1
   Result: companion offers work, not trust.


Guarded State
-------------

Condition:
Relationship below 20.

Opening:
"You are alive, armed, and asking questions. Only one of those is automatically useful."

Options:

1. "Then teach me how to help."
   Relationship: +2
   Result: companion approves learning before swinging.

2. "I am listening."
   Relationship: +1
   Result: companion says listening after inconvenience is the test.

3. "I survive well enough."
   Relationship: 0
   Result: companion says survival is only proof of another chance.

4. "I do not have time for this."
   Relationship: -1
   Result: companion tells the player to spend haste elsewhere.


Oathstead Branch
----------------

This branch appears if the companion is recruited or stationed at Oathstead.

Root option:
"Talk about Oathstead"

Opening:

If stationed:
"I thought a camp would feel temporary. This one keeps asking people to become less temporary with it."

If not stationed but recruited:
"Oathstead is not polished enough to lie well. I find that almost comforting."

Options:

1. "Does this place feel like yours?"
   Relationship: +2
   Result: companion-specific home line.

2. "How is your work here?"
   Relationship: +1
   Result: assigned building / worker role line.

3. "What does Oathstead need next?"
   Relationship: +1
   Result: companion-specific need line.

4. "Back to topics"
   Relationship: 0
   Result: root.


Oathstead Assigned Building Lines
---------------------------------

If the companion is assigned to a building:

Greeting:
"Oathstead has put me at the [building]. I am making it useful in my own way."

Work line:
"The [building] gives me [role]. It is good to have hands busy with something that builds instead of only survives."

If no building is assigned:

"Give me a place to work and I will make it earn its roof."

Relationship Effects
--------------------

Dialogue options can change relationship.

Positive changes may be improved by player Charisma.

Special companion NPC positive gains are softened so trust grows more slowly and feels earned.

Effect strings:

* romance:start
  Adds companion to romancedCompanions.

* romance:end
  Removes companion from romancedCompanions.

* marriage:accept
  Adds companion to romancedCompanions and marriedCompanions.


Design Notes
------------

The goal is not to make every companion give long speeches.

The goal is to make every click answer a logical question:

* What is happening?
* Why does it matter to this character?
* What should I do?
* What happens if I support, doubt, or rush them?
* How has the relationship changed?
* How does Oathstead change what they say?

This should make companions feel like people whose trust is built in layers, not vendors who repeat disconnected quotes.
